Culture · Games — Launch Day

The 13-Year Wait Ends: GTA VI Takes the World

By the Ledger's Wire Staff · Wellington & New York, Thursday

The wait lasted 13 years; it ended at midnight. Grand Theft Auto VI went on sale at midnight local time on Thursday, rolling out from New Zealand around the world for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — the first new GTA since 2013. Critics are close to unanimous: a Metacritic score of 99 from more than 150 reviews ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time as the joint-highest-rated game ever. Sensor Tower estimates some 17 million copies and $1.65 billion in sales before launch day had even begun.

Shenzhen · The APEC Summit

Three Presidents, One Table in Shenzhen

The leaders of the United States, China and Russia sat down together for the first time on Thursday, on the sidelines of the APEC summit. Trump, Xi and Putin talked for some 90 minutes on Ukraine and nuclear arms, agreed to open a dialogue on reducing nuclear risks, and declared jointly that "a nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought." For Putin, it was a diplomatic comeback, five years after the invasion of Ukraine made him a pariah.

New York · Markets

Anthropic's $81 Billion Debut Stuns Wall Street

Anthropic, the AI lab behind the Claude chatbot, began trading on the Nasdaq on Thursday under the ticker ANT, after pricing the largest IPO on record: 980 million shares at $83, raising about $81 billion and valuing the company near $1.5 trillion. The shares opened at $111.50 and closed at $97.17, up 17 percent — briefly a $2 trillion company.

Bay of Bengal · Weather

Third Cyclone in a Month Bears Down on India

Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan churned toward India's east coast on Thursday, the third cyclone in a month to threaten the region. The India Meteorological Department put the storm about 420 km east-southeast of Narsapur and forecast landfall near Gopalpur, Odisha, around noon on Friday, with winds of 90–100 km/h gusting to 110. Odisha evacuated some 50,000 people under red rain warnings.

New Delhi · Royalty

William and Kate Wrap Up India Tour

The Prince and Princess of Wales closed their three-day tour of India — their first joint overseas trip in four years — with talks with Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi, who praised William's Earthshot Prize after its Mumbai awards night. The couple also played cricket with Sachin Tendulkar.

The Hague · Justice

World Court: Myanmar Committed Genocide

The International Court of Justice ruled on Thursday that Myanmar's government committed genocide against the Rohingya people in 2017 — a landmark judgment in the case brought by The Gambia in 2019, and a victory for the hundreds of thousands of Rohingya driven from their homes.
